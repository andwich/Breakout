Here's the full breakdown across all three areas. A few things worth calling out in priority order:

**Most impactful to fix first:**

The double-coloring bug in `level_builder.gd` is likely actively breaking your color palette right now. When you set both `brick.modulate` and `_sprite.color` to the same neon color, Godot multiplies them channel-by-channel — so cyan (0.2, 1.0, 1.0) becomes (0.04, 1.0, 1.0), a completely different shade. Remove the `brick.modulate = ROW_COLORS[row]` line from level_builder and let `brick.gd` own all coloring through `row_color`.

The `LEVEL_INTRO` phase guard is a real edge-case bug — if `_disable_sticky` fires on a timer expiry and launches a ball during the intro, it can exit immediately and decrement lives on the next level.

**Mechanics refinements** worth doing before polish: The global 2-second powerup cooldown is punishing players who earned it with multiball — dropping it to ~0.8s still prevents the spam scenario while letting chains feel rewarding. The paddle/wall boundary mismatch is cosmetic but players will notice the paddle creeping behind the wall neon line.

**On the aesthetic side**, the two medium-priority items (powerup drift/rotation, boss glow shader vs scale conflict) will have the biggest visible impact. The HUD timer warning flash is a nice UX touch that shouldn't take long to add.
